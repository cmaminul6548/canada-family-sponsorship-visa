## 📌 Eligibility & Categories

### 👥 Who Can Sponsor?
* Canadian citizens or permanent residents (18+ years old).
* Must meet financial requirements to support sponsored relatives.

### 📋 Who Can Be Sponsored?
* **Spouse & Partners:** Married, common-law, or conjugal partners.
* **Children:** Dependent unmarried children under 22.
* **Parents & Grandparents:** Via the PGP program or Super Visa.


## 🛠️ Quick Step Process

1. **Check Eligibility:** Confirm both parties qualify.
2. **Gather Documents:** Identity papers, financial proofs, and relationship certificates.
3. **Fill Forms & Pay Fees:** Complete IRCC application forms and pay processing fees online.
4. **Submit Application:** File online via the official IRCC portal.
5. **Biometrics & Medical:** Attend tests when requested.
